Routing to D:\ Drive or /user/ Custom Folders

By browser security design, Chromium (Chrome & Edge) locks all extension downloads inside the Downloads directory. Use native Windows Directory Junctions (folder aliases) to seamlessly direct downloads anywhere on your PC.

How It Works: Windows Junctions

A Directory Junction acts like a high-speed portal inside your Downloads folder. When you create a junction called Costume or DriveD, anything Chrome saves into Downloads/Costume/ or Downloads/DriveD/ is physically stored on C:\user\costume or D:\ without duplicating space or violating browser store sandboxes!

1-Click Command Generator

New-Item -ItemType Junction -Path "$HOME\Downloads\DriveD" -Target "D:\"

Alternative: Change Master Download Folder in Browser Settings

You can also change your browser's primary download location from C:\Users\KBSV\Downloads to D:\ directly:

  • In Microsoft Edge: Open edge://settings/downloads → click Change under "Location" → select D:\.
  • In Google Chrome: Open chrome://settings/downloads → click Change under "Location" → select D:\.
